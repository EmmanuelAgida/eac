Who we are

EAC — Emmanuel Agida Consult is an integrated strategic advisory and execution firm committed to helping businesses, governments, investors and institutions navigate complexity, strengthen organisational performance and deliver consequential projects.

Our approach connects intelligence with decision-making and strategy with implementation. We help clients understand complex challenges, identify opportunities, develop practical responses, and coordinate the people, resources and partnerships required to deliver agreed objectives.

Operating from Nigeria with an African outlook, EAC is building the expertise, partnerships and institutional capabilities to serve clients across sectors. Our delivery model combines a lean core with appropriately qualified associates and partners selected for each engagement.

Vision

To become a trusted African-origin strategic advisory and execution firm recognised for intellectual rigour, institutional credibility, and the capacity to help organisations navigate complexity and achieve consequential results.

Mission

To equip businesses, governments, investors and institutions with the strategic intelligence, professional advisory and execution capabilities required to solve complex problems, strengthen organisational performance and advance sustainable growth.

Value proposition

We help organisations make better strategic decisions and deliver complex objectives by integrating intelligence, strategy and execution.

Core capabilities

Three connected capabilities that take a client from understanding a problem to delivering the result.

Intelligence

Research, strategic analysis, market and policy intelligence, stakeholder mapping, institutional assessment, and risk and opportunity analysis.

Strategy

Corporate and organisational strategy, business planning, policy advisory, institutional development, transformation planning, and performance improvement.

Execution

Project and programme delivery, implementation planning, stakeholder coordination, strategic communications, performance monitoring, and delivery assurance.

Corporate communications strategy; public and media relations; executive positioning and thought leadership; stakeholder engagement; reputation and crisis communication advisory; public awareness and advocacy campaigns; communications for programmes and initiatives; corporate narrative and messaging; event communications and strategic visibility.
